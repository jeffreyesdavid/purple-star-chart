# Purple Star Chart 紫微斗數

**Zi Wei Dou Shu (Purple Star Astrology) in English.** Enter a birth date and two-hour birth block to place 100+ stars across the 12 palaces of life, then read each palace in plain English.

🌐 **Live site:** https://jeffreyesdavid.github.io/purple-star-chart/

## Features

- Full 12-palace chart in the traditional 4×4 layout, with Chinese star names plus English names
- Plain-English readings for every palace and all 14 main stars: strengths, watch-outs, love, money, and work
- Star strength (Peak → Fallen) and what it means in each palace
- The Four Transformations (祿 Luck · 權 Power · 科 Fame · 忌 Obstacle) for your birth year
- Timing: your current 10-year decade and any year you choose
- "Big picture" summary of your chart
- Gregorian or lunar birth dates
- Runs fully in your browser; your birth info never leaves your device

## Run it locally

It's a single HTML file. Open `index.html` in any browser.

## Disclaimer

Readings are a cultural and reflective guide, not predictions to base decisions on. Birth hour uses local clock time without true-solar-time correction.
